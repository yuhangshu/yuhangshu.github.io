Teaching & mentorship

I have been very fortunate to teach and mentor across the undergraduate curriculum at UVA, from research methods and statistics to courses in developmental, social, and gender psychology, as well as guest lectures on moral development.

Mentoring has been one of the most rewarding parts of my graduate training. Through my work in the Early Social Development Lab, I have had the opportunity to mentor more than 25 undergraduate research assistants and honors students. I especially enjoy watching students grow from learning the basics of research to confidently discussing their own projects, presenting their work, and recognizing the skills they have developed along the way.

What I value most is seeing students gradually discover what questions genuinely interest them, explore where those interests might lead, and begin working toward goals of their own. I am very grateful to be able to support them in that process and to play even a small role as they build confidence, develop as researchers, and figure out what they want to pursue next.
